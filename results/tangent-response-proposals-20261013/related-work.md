No fetched or uploaded code was executed, no numerical experiment was rerun, and no project source or scientific artifact was changed.

Low rank, conditioning, operator learning, Duhamel expansion, and their combination are established ingredients.

## The interaction-picture proposal is an established identity, not a new derivation

For the registered finite equation, write the same-path tangent generator as

`A_a(t) = A_mean(t) + H_a(t)`, with `H_a(t)z = -2 r B(a(t)-mean(a(t)), z)`.

The repository analysis already derives

`U_a(t,s) = G_a(t,s) + integral_s^t G_a(t,q) H_a(q) U_a(q,s) dq`.

One substitution gives the first interaction term

`T1(t,s) = integral_s^t G_a(t,q) H_a(q) G_a(q,s) dq`.

Approximating `U_a-G_a-T1` is a new **experimental representation choice** for this program; Duhamel iteration and the interaction picture are prior art. Blanes et al. explicitly discuss Dyson time-ordered products and interaction-picture changes of variables [1]. These should not be confused with a Magnus exponential of nested commutators: the expansions organize the same evolution differently.

For FD, `H_a` is spatial multiplication times the registered coefficient. In dealiased same-grid Galerkin, the product must remain the target's projected/dealiased bilinear `B`; replacing it with an aliased nodal product or assuming product associativity changes the contract.

A local or heat-sandwiched term need not have low matrix rank. For small positive lag, the formal leading correction is `(t-s)H_a(s)`, which can act on many independent directions. On each fixed finite grid all matrices are technically compact, so the relevant claim is a **small numerical rank at a stated tolerance and lag**, not abstract compactness. Diffusive smoothing at longer lag is motivation to measure the remainder, not a guarantee. Interaction transforms involving an inverse heat semigroup can also amplify high frequencies; implementing the stable integral form and measuring conditioning is preferable to assuming that a formal change of variables is numerically benign.

## Precise claims worth testing

1. **Conditioning attribution:** on the original registered probe family, a training-only equilibrated solve substantially reduces physical action error without changing factor rank, and retains its behavior after FP32 deployment. This diagnoses a fitted representation; it does not authorize reclassifying old B.
2. **Local-remainder compression:** on a newly frozen finite target and lag menu, `T1` plus a conditioned rank-at-most-eight remainder meets the unchanged declared held-out action and endpoint-gain criteria at a lower complete cost than a sufficiently accurate tangent-action alternative.
3. **Response-target learning:** with the same training information, explicit residual/path decoding and forcing linearity improve held-out endpoint accuracy/cost relative to matched FNO/DeepONet-derived controls and a generic residual learner.
4. **Qualified amortization:** repeated independent actions on one fully specified path permit measured encoding reuse; autonomous steps require re-encoding. The benefit survives reference acquisition, fitting, bridges, quadrature and complete inference costs.

Each statement names the target, allowed information, comparator and falsification test. None claims to be the first use of a neural encoder, a separable kernel, a physics constraint, a defect correction, a heat factor, or a regularized solve. A broader novelty claim requires a subsequent systematic search and closer comparison to the nearest full papers.
